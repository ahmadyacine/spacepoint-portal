# SpacePoint Internship & Instructor Program - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. Intern vs. Instructor: Key Differences

### Intern
An intern joins SpacePoint to gain practical experience, contribute to projects, learn from the team, and develop professional skills in STEM education, operations, technology, and space-related programs.

### Instructor
An instructor is an approved member of the SpacePoint Instructor Network who is qualified to deliver SpacePoint workshops, events, and educational programs.

Instructor roles are paid on a part-time freelance basis and are compensated per workshop, event, or project assignment.

---

## 2. Is the internship paid?

**No.**

The SpacePoint Internship Program is an educational and professional development opportunity designed to provide real-world experience, mentorship, and exposure to industry projects.

---

## 3. Are instructors paid?

**Yes.**

Approved instructors work with SpacePoint on a freelance/part-time basis and are compensated when delivering workshops, events, training sessions, or other assigned activities.

---

## 4. Do interns and instructors follow the same onboarding process?

**Yes.**

Both interns and instructors must successfully complete the onboarding process through the SpacePoint Portal.

---

## 5. What is the onboarding process?

The onboarding process consists of the following phases:

### Phase 1 – Learning Modules
- Watch the assigned onboarding videos.
- Submit summaries for each video.

### Phase 2 – SpacePoint Research Task
- Complete the assigned SpacePoint.Terms research activity.

### Phase 3 – Presentation
- Prepare and deliver a presentation based on the research task.

### Phase 4 – Evaluation
- The SpacePoint team reviews all submitted work and presentations.

Applicants who successfully complete all phases become eligible to join the SpacePoint Instructor Network.

## 21. What happens after I complete the onboarding process and become an Instructor?

After successfully completing all onboarding requirements and being approved as an Instructor, you will be invited to attend a dedicated CubeSat Instructor Training Session.

The training typically includes:

- Introduction to the SpacePoint CubeSat Kit (SatKit)
- Workshop delivery methodology
- Hands-on activities and demonstrations
- Instructor expectations and best practices
- Student engagement techniques
- Program materials and resources

The training session is usually around **2 hours** and will be scheduled individually. Training materials will also be shared with approved instructors.

---

## 22. Will I immediately start delivering workshops after approval?

**Not necessarily.**

Workshop assignments depend on:

- Upcoming programs
- Partner requirements
- Workshop locations
- Instructor availability
- Program schedules

Approved instructors will be contacted whenever suitable opportunities become available.

---

## 23. How are instructors selected for workshops?

Workshop assignments are coordinated based on:

1. The Emirate where the workshop is taking place.
2. Instructor availability.
3. Relevant experience and training.
4. First response / first availability confirmation.

Priority is generally given to qualified instructors located in the same Emirate as the workshop whenever possible.
